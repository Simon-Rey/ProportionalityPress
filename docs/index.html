Showcasing the Fair Selection of Comments

What if there was a better way to summarize the comments section of a news article than simply showing the most liked or the newest comments? That’s the question behind the Proportionality Press. This website explores practical methods for selecting representative comments that go beyond just sorting by date or number of likes.

At the Proportionality Press, comments are chosen in a proportional way. This means we don’t just highlight the most popular opinions, but also represent significant groups with differing viewpoints. Our goal: a rich and balanced conversation under your next Reddit post.

For each article, you can explore different comment selections created by various methods, along with explanations of what these approaches reveal.
