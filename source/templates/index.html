Showcasing the Fair Selection of Comments

What if there was a better way to summarize the comment section of a news article than simply showing the most liked or the newest comments? That's the question behind the Proportionality Press. This website explores practical methods for selecting representative comments that go beyond just sorting by date or number of likes. Our goal fostering a rich and balanced conversation under your next Reddit post.

We present here different articles for which users could submit comment and react (via a thumb-up button) to other comments. For each article, you can explore several comment selections and explore what is the impact of the different approaches.
