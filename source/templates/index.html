Welcome to the Proportionality Press

The Proportionality Press explores how we can improve collective decisions and public conversations using clear, principled approaches. This website showcases practical approaches to selecting content that reflects a broad range of perspectives.

We focus on the selection of representative comments from online discussions, — a Reddit thread or the comment section beneath a news article for instance. Rather than simply highlighting only the most liked or most recent contributions, we apply voting-based methods to explore different ways of selecting representative comments.

For each article, we present selections produced by different methods and analyze their practical implications. You can start by reading one of our featured articles, or simply browse through the full list.
